About this Beta and its coverage

Based on the MB-800 objectives effective June 30, 2026. Reviewed September 9, 2026, with representative scenarios across all 22 skill areas. This does not exhaust every setting or replace hands-on study, and is not a complete exam simulation.

Random Practice draws 40–45 questions. The 120-minute timer, 70% practice target, and readiness indicators are study tools, not a prediction of the real exam score or length. No standalone case studies are included in this first Beta.

Use Feedback to report ambiguous wording or answers. References are shown with explanations after answering in Practice mode or in the results in Test mode.

Earlier saved attempts and scores are preserved. Start a fresh attempt to include the expanded bank; an older score may cover fewer questions.
